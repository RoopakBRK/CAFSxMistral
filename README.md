# CAFS — Certificate Anti-Forgery System

CAFS (Certificate Anti-Forgery System) is an AI-powered system designed to verify the authenticity of digital certificates and detect potential forgery or tampering.

### What It Does

CAFS analyzes uploaded certificates and checks their contents for signs of manipulation, inconsistencies, or suspicious alterations. It helps organizations quickly determine whether a certificate appears genuine or potentially forged.

The system combines document processing, verification logic, and AI-based analysis to make certificate validation faster, more reliable, and easier to scale.

It can be used for certificate verification in **education, recruitment, compliance, and other document-heavy workflows**.
